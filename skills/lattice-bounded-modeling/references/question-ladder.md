# Question Ladder

Use a short sequence of questions to surface the shape of the slice before any modeling work begins.

Suggested order:
1. What outcome should this model support?
2. What slice or subsystem are we focusing on first?
3. What baseline, comparison, or relative relationship matters, if any?
4. What kind of deeper analysis should the model support?
5. What is explicitly out of scope for this pass?
6. What should happen when data or evidence is incomplete, missing, or not comparable?

Rules for asking:
- keep the sequence short and focused
- ask one question at a time unless the user has already answered enough
- do not jump into units until the boundary is stable
- if a later answer changes the boundary, restart the ladder from the relevant question

The ladder is meant to surface decisions, not to produce the model itself.
